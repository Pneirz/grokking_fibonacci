# Figure 2 provenance

`fig_prediction_profiles.pdf`, `fig_prediction_profiles.svg`, and
`fig_prediction_profiles.png` are generated by
`scripts/plot_prediction_profiles.py` from the archived final
`outputs/confirm_*/predictions.csv` files.

The figure uses the per-index median of the ten saved seeds for five selected
models: MLP 2x512, Product MLP, MultKAN, NALU, and the two-dimensional
recurrent control. The left panel plots a sign-preserving
`log10(abs(value) + 1)` transform of the raw prediction and Fibonacci target.
The right panel plots the per-index median of saved relative absolute error
for indices 31 through 60. The colour bands denote the near (31--35), mid
(36--45), and far (46--60) evaluation ranges; the dashed horizontal line is
the far-success threshold of `5e-2`.

The script does not train models or alter experiment outputs. It is a plotting
step over the archived CSV records. The PDF and SVG are vector assets. The PNG
is rendered at 1200 dpi at the manuscript's 122 mm figure width.

See `FIGURE_VERIFICATION.md` for the reconstruction scope and its comparison
with the legacy figure.
