# Figure 1 provenance

`fig_short_dynamics.pdf`, `fig_short_dynamics.svg`, and
`fig_short_dynamics.png` are generated by `scripts/plot_short_dynamics.py`
from the archived `curves.csv` files in all nine `outputs/confirm_*` run
directories.

For every model, evaluation step, and error band, the line is the median over
the ten saved seeds and the shaded region spans the first and third quartiles.
The four panels report train, near, mid, and far mean relative absolute error.
The dashed line marks `1e-2` in the train panel and `5e-2` in each future-band
panel.

The command below performs only aggregation and rendering over archived CSV
records; it does not train models or alter results. The `--publication` option
sets the final width to 122 mm. PDF and SVG are vector assets; PNG is rendered
at 1200 dpi.
